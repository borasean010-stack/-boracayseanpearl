"Experience the scenic heartbeat of Boracay"

While the ocean is Boracay's soul, its land tells a story of vibrant culture and stunning geography. Our land tour is designed to show you the best viewpoints, the most tranquil beaches, and the local way of life, all from the comfort of our comfortable transport.

Sky-High Views

Stand atop Mt. Luho and watch the world go by. From this vantage point, you can see the entire span of Boracay, from the northern tip down to the southern shores, with the Sibuyan Sea and Tablas Strait on either side.

Pristine Puka

Puka Shell Beach is a refreshing change from the bustling Station 2. Relax under a coconut tree, swim in the deep blue waters, and shop for authentic puka shell souvenirs from the local vendors.

The Keyhole

A masterpiece of nature, the Boracay Keyhole at Newcoast is a must-visit. This giant rock formation with a hole in the middle offers a perfect framing for the ocean, creating a magical backdrop for your memories.

Local Life

Pass through local villages and see the daily life of the islanders. Our guides will share stories about Boracay's history and its transformation into a world-class destination.
