Secure Your Date

"The ultimate and most luxurious way to experience Boracay’s ocean"

Step away from ordinary speedboats and experience a whole new level of island hopping! Our signature luxury yacht features a wide deck, a scenic second-floor rooftop, and a clean onboard restroom, ensuring a comfortable and premium experience for couples, friends, and families alike.

Premium Snorkeling

We drop anchor at Boracay’s most beautiful, marine-rich snorkeling points. As you swim among vibrant coral reefs and tropical fish, our dedicated crew will dive in with a GoPro to capture your magical underwater moments.

Floating Playground

Thrill-seekers can slide straight into the ocean from our massive onboard water slide or make a splash from the diving platform. For a more relaxed pace, enjoy a peaceful ride on our complimentary kayaks and stand-up paddleboards.

Endless Treats

Indulge in a delightful onboard spread featuring unlimited signature cocktails, juices, and mineral water, accompanied by fresh seasonal fruits, local snacks, and a comforting bowl of hot Korean ramen.

Multilingual Crew

Stay safe and entertained with our energetic, professional local crew who speak both English and Korean. High-energy music and spectacular dance performances by our talented crew will be the highlight of your trip.
